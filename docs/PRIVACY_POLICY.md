This Privacy Policy applies to Ads DaseRu Kun (internal nickname: X広告出せる君), a conversational interface for preparing, reviewing, approving, executing, and reporting X Ads operations.

## 2. Information processed

Depending on the features a Client uses, the service may process:

- X Ads account, campaign, line-item, targeting, creative/Post, funding, delivery, billing, and analytics data made available through the X Ads API;
- advertising specifications supplied by the Client, such as product/service information, destination URLs, campaign goals, budgets, schedules, targeting, creative selections, and stop conditions;
- X-native conversion and website-event reporting data when the Client has enabled applicable X measurement features;
- limited technical and security records required to execute, audit, diagnose, or protect the service;
- support communications and information the Client voluntarily provides for setup or troubleshooting.

X passwords, OAuth consumer secrets, access tokens, access-token secrets, or equivalent credentials must not be pasted into ChatGPT conversations, GitHub Issues, comments, screenshots, or customer-facing reports. Such credentials belong only in the dedicated deployment's secret store or authorization flow.

## 3. Purposes of processing

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

- provide the requested X Ads campaign-management workflow;
- prepare and display proposed ad settings before execution;
- enforce approval, execution, account-pinning, and budget controls;
- perform X Ads API operations explicitly authorized by the Client;
- provide X-native reporting and calculate the separately disclosed Company Service fee;
- provide support, diagnose failures, prevent misuse, and maintain security;
- comply with applicable law and contractual obligations.

The service does not use one Client's X Ads Data to provide another Client with access to that data.

## 4. Client isolation

The initial beta uses a dedicated-deployment model: **one Client = one dedicated deployment + one secret store + one pinned X Ads account**.

Customer X Materials, credentials, reporting data, and operational records are not intentionally combined into a shared cross-customer data store during this beta architecture.

## 5. Third parties and processors

Information may be transmitted to or processed by services required to provide the requested functionality, including:

- X, for X Ads API access, campaign management, reporting, and measurement;
- infrastructure and development services used to operate the Client's dedicated deployment;
- payment or billing providers when needed to charge the separately disclosed Company Service fee.

The service does not sell Client X Ads Data or personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## 6. Retention and deletion

X Materials and Client operational data are retained only while reasonably necessary for the approved workflow, support, billing, security, legal obligations, or dispute handling, and are deleted when no longer required, subject to applicable law and the then-current X contractual requirements.

A Client may end use of the service and revoke the connected X application. Offboarding includes disabling/removing the dedicated deployment and removing applicable credentials and retained operational data that are no longer required.

## 7. Client control and disconnection

Clients retain direct control of their X Ads account through X Ads Manager. They may disassociate the service by revoking the connected application in X and disabling/removing their dedicated deployment. The operator will not intentionally prevent or delay such disassociation.

Requests concerning access, correction, deletion, or other privacy matters may be sent to **yamauchi.rts.office@gmail.com**. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law, technical feasibility, and contractual retention requirements.

## 8. Security

The service uses safeguards including dedicated Client isolation, secret-store separation, pinned Ads account writes, a master write switch, budget ceilings, command-bound approval controls, a separate short-lived execution key, and post-write read-back where available.

If an incident may expose X Materials or Client information, the operator will take reasonable steps to stop affected access, rotate or revoke credentials where appropriate, assess the incident, preserve necessary evidence, remediate the issue, and make notices required by applicable law or the then-current X agreement.

## 9. Measurement and conversion data

If a Client enables X Pixel, website event tracking, conversion measurement, or other tracking technologies, the Client is responsible for ensuring that its own website, notices, consent mechanisms, and use of those technologies comply with applicable law and X policies. The service will provide required integration information but does not replace the Client's own privacy and consent obligations.

## 10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or X platform requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
